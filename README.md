Entry for [make.fun](https://make.fun/bounty/u-motasimfuad-have-you-tried-turning-it-off-154a938b): one funny line a hopeless tech-support agent says to a desperate customer.

> I've turned it off and on again three times now — honestly, at this point I'm just stalling until my shift ends.

The program writes the line to `/output/entry.txt`.

Why this line: the competition title already says "have you tried turning it off", so repeating the classic would lose on freshness. This line twists it instead — the ritual is already spent (three times), the agent is out of script, and his honest confession is that he is just stalling until his shift ends. The humour is the agent's cheerful uselessness, the customer's desperation is felt rather than explained, and at 112 chars it also helps the smaller-text tie-break.
